Trade and tariffs

Since Jul 2026Nation Thailand · Bangkok Post

12.5% US tariff now applies to Thai garments and textiles

The US placed a Section 301 tariff, linked to forced-labour rules, on most Thai imports from July 24, 2026, replacing the earlier reciprocal rate. Some sources still quote 19% or 36%, so check USTR or CBP for the official rate.

Market near USD 5 billion, growing slowly

One estimate puts the market at about USD 4.97 billion in 2026, growing about 1.6% a year to 2031. Rising wages and about 23,000 unfilled jobs, as workers move to electronics and car plants, add to cost pressure. Dyeing and wastewater compliance also push costs up.
