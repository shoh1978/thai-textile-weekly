[Past / จัดไปแล้ว 10–11 Oct] Thai national costume and craft demonstrations in Paris
The Department of Cultural Promotion held "Chud Thai: Knowledge, Craftsmanship and Practices of the Thai National Costume" at Le Meurice, Paris, on 10–11 October. Princess Sirivannavari presided over a seminar on the evolution of Thai dress and textiles, and craftspeople demonstrated sadeng embroidery, yok brocade weaving, gold-thread work and more. The report adds that Thailand's nomination of Thai costume for UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list is due to be considered at the committee's 21st session in December 2026 (Xiamen, China). (MGR Online, 17 Oct)
ชุดไทยและงานช่างฝีมือไทยโชว์ที่ปารีส

Craft spotlight / เจาะลึกงานฝีมือ
Yok weaving and sadeng embroidery: court crafts in Paris
Two of the techniques demonstrated in Paris were yok (supplementary-weft brocade weaving) and sadeng embroidery, an elite court technique. National Artist Weeratham Trakulngoenthai led both demonstrations, alongside gold-thread work, embroidery and ancient jewellery making. A reminder that these slow, labour-intensive skills sit at the heart of Thailand's UNESCO bid for Thai costume.
ทอผ้ายกและปักสะดึงกรึงไหม งานช่างราชสำนักที่ไปไกลถึงปารีส
